# Quillon Bay Tinkerers' Guild: Events for 2026-27

This organization is fictional. It exists only as sample data for a workshop.

## Fall Intake Night

Wednesday, September 16, 2026, at 6:00 PM in the Marrow Hall atrium. New members can sign up and earn a Green Card on the spot.

## The Soggy Bridge Challenge

Saturday, October 24, 2026, at 1:00 PM in the Boiler. Teams of two build a bridge from craft sticks and white glue only, then test it over a tank of water. A bridge must span 60 cm and hold 12 kg for 30 seconds. The lightest bridge that passes wins the Golden Wrench. Entry costs $5 per team.

## Winter Repair Cafe

Saturday, December 5, 2026, from 11:00 AM to 3:00 PM in the Marrow Hall atrium. Guild members fix broken items for other students at no charge. Donations go to the Boiler tool fund.

## Spring Showcase

Saturday, April 10, 2027, from 12:00 PM to 5:00 PM in the Marrow Hall atrium. Members show finished projects. Showing a project here is one of the requirements for Arc level.

## Annual General Meeting

Wednesday, April 28, 2027, at 7:15 PM in the Boiler. Members elect the Guild Chair, the Treasurer and the Head Steward for the next academic year.
