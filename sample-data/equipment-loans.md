# Quillon Bay Tinkerers' Guild: Equipment Loans

This organization is fictional. It exists only as sample data for a workshop.

## How to borrow

The Loan Desk is open Tuesday and Thursday from 5:00 PM to 7:00 PM, at the front of the Boiler. Sign the Red Ledger when you take an item and again when you return it. A member can have at most two items out at one time.

## What you can borrow

| Item | Loan period | Deposit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Oscilloscope (Big Ada) | 3 days | $20 |
| Soldering kit | 7 days | None |
| Multimeter | 14 days | None |
| Bench power supply | 7 days | $10 |

The Guild's 3D printer, Wobbly Pete, cannot be borrowed. It leans to the left and only works on its own table.

## Late returns

The late fee is $1.50 per day, per item. The fee stops at $15 per item. The Guild keeps any deposit until the late fee is paid.

An item that is more than 30 days late counts as lost. The member then pays the replacement cost and cannot borrow anything for the rest of the academic year.

## Damage

Tell the Loan Desk about any damage when you return an item. Normal wear costs nothing. The Treasurer decides what a member pays for other damage.
