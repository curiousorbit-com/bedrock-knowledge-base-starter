# Quillon Bay Tinkerers' Guild: Member Handbook

This organization is fictional. It exists only as sample data for a workshop.

## About the Guild

The Quillon Bay Tinkerers' Guild is a student club at Quillon Bay College. It was founded in 2011 by four students who repaired a broken vending machine and kept the leftover parts. The Guild's workshop is in the old boiler room in the basement of Marrow Hall, Room B14. Members call it the Boiler.

The Guild's mascot is a one-eyed otter named Sprocket. A stuffed Sprocket sits on the shelf above the workshop door. Moving Sprocket off that shelf costs the guilty member one batch of cookies, payable at the next meeting.

## Membership levels

Any registered Quillon Bay College student can join. Members move through three levels.

- **Spark:** every new member starts here. A Spark can use hand tools after earning a Green Card.
- **Filament:** a Spark becomes a Filament after attending three build sessions and passing the safety quiz.
- **Arc:** a Filament becomes an Arc after showing a finished project at the Spring Showcase and volunteering for 10 hours at Guild events.

Only an Arc can serve as a Steward. Stewards supervise the workshop during open hours.

## Dues

Dues are $18 per term. Dues must be paid by the Thursday of the third week of term. A member who has not paid by that date loses workshop access until the dues are paid.

Pay dues to the Treasurer at any meeting.

## Meetings and decisions

The Guild meets every second Wednesday at 7:15 PM in the Boiler. A vote needs at least 15 members present. Any purchase over $250 needs a vote of the members. The Treasurer can approve smaller purchases alone.

## The Last Tinker rule

The last member to leave the Boiler each night is the Last Tinker. The Last Tinker switches off the main breaker, checks that the laser cutter is cold, and rings the brass bell twice so that anyone in the back room knows the lights are going out.
