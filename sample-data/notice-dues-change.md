# Notice to Members: Dues Change

This organization is fictional. It exists only as sample data for a workshop.

Date of notice: Tuesday, November 3, 2026

From: Ruben Achterhof, Treasurer

At the meeting on October 28, 2026, members voted 31 to 6 to raise dues. Starting with the term that begins on Monday, January 11, 2027, dues are $24 per term. This notice replaces the dues amount in the Member Handbook.

The increase pays for a new laser tube, which costs $1,140. The payment deadline does not change. Late fees for equipment loans do not change.
