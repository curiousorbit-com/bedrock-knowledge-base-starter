# Quillon Bay Tinkerers' Guild: Workshop Rules

This organization is fictional. It exists only as sample data for a workshop.

## Opening hours

The Boiler is open Monday to Thursday from 4:00 PM to 10:00 PM and Saturday from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM. It is closed on Friday and Sunday. A Steward must be on duty before anyone can switch on a power tool.

## Safety cards

Each tool needs a safety card. The Head Steward issues all cards.

- **Green Card:** hand tools and soldering irons. Earned in a 30-minute session.
- **Orange Card:** the drill press and the bandsaw. Earned in a 2-hour session. Requires a Green Card.
- **Red Card:** the laser cutter. Earned in a 90-minute session. Requires an Orange Card and Filament level.

## What everyone wears

Safety glasses and closed shoes are required at all times. Long hair must be tied back. There is a jar of free hair ties on the workbench by the sink.

## Laser cutter rules

- Never leave the laser cutter running unattended, even for one minute.
- Never cut PVC or vinyl. They release chlorine gas.
- A single job can run for 45 minutes at most. Let the machine cool for 15 minutes before starting another job.
- The fire blanket hangs on the east wall beside the door. Members call it Gerald.

## Project storage

Unfinished projects go on the Project Shelf with a tag showing the owner's name and the date. A project can stay on the Project Shelf for 21 days. After 21 days it moves to the Purgatory Shelf for 7 more days. After that, its parts go into the recycling bins for anyone to use.

## Incidents and strikes

Report every injury, fire or damaged tool to the Head Steward within 24 hours. A member who breaks a safety rule receives a strike. A member who receives three strikes in one academic year loses workshop access for 30 days.
